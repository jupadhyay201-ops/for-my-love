A letter for My Love

Read this whenever it feels right. No pressure, just honesty.

Meri My Love, ❤️

I’m genuinely sorry. Mujhe pata hai ki maine apni baaton aur apni kuch cheezon se tujhe bahut hurt kiya hai. Main kabhi nahi chahta tha ki meri wajah se tujhe dard ho, lekin main samajhta hoon ki meri intention jo bhi thi, meri baaton ka asar tujh par hua — aur uske liye mujhe dil se maafi chahiye.

Maine jo bhi kiya aur jo bhi tujhe kaha, uske liye I’m really sorry. Main apni galti ko lightly nahi lena chahta. Main aage se zyada sochunga, teri feelings ko dhyaan se samjhunga aur koshish karunga ki apni galtiyon ko sirf words se nahi, apne actions se bhi sudhaarun.

Tu mere liye bahut special hai, Ayushi. Main tujhse bohot, bohot zyada pyaar karta hoon. Tere saath hone se meri duniya aur achhi lagti hai. Kabhi-kabhi main ye baat sahi tareeke se dikha nahi paata, par iska matlab ye nahi ki tu mere liye kam important hai. Tu hai toh aisa lagta hai ki mere paas bahut kuch hai.

Hamare beech ladai ho gayi, iska matlab ye nahi ki hamari achhi memories ya ek-doosre ke liye feelings khatam ho jaati hain. Main chahta hoon ki hum dono apni baat honestly keh sakein, ek-doosre ko samajhne ki koshish karein aur jab galti ho toh usse accept bhi karein. Main tere saath rehna chahta hoon aur hamare rishte ko aur achha banana chahta hoon.

Main ye nahi keh raha ki tu turant sab bhool ja ya abhi hi mujhe maaf kar de. Teri feelings important hain, aur agar tujhe time chahiye toh main uski respect karunga. Bas jab tu ready ho, main chahta hoon ki hum araam se baat karein. Main tujhe sunna chahta hoon — bina argue kiye, bina teri feelings ko chhota samjhe.

Please mujhe maaf kar dena, jab tera dil kahe. I’m so sorry, My Love. I love you — forever and ever. ❤️

Tu hai toh sab kuch hai mere paas. 🤍

With all my love,
Jatin ✦

A sorry is a beginning. I want my actions to show you that I mean it.
